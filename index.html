What this is

The Space Resources Roundtable (SRR) has met at the Colorado School of Mines — and earlier at other venues — since 1999, and is one of the longest-running forums on lunar, asteroid and Martian resource utilization. Its proceedings are public on isruinfo.com but spread over two decades of pages with no search. This site is an index over that material.

What it is not

  • This is an independent research tool. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Space Resources Roundtable, the Colorado School of Mines, LPI/USRA or isruinfo.com.
  • No presentation files are hosted or mirrored. Every result links to the original source.
  • Themes are assigned automatically from titles by keyword rules and will sometimes be wrong. "General" means no rule matched.
  • "Find other public sources" uses an AI model with web search. Its suggestions are unverified — check them before citing.
